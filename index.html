Structure Roadmap Instructions

Reveal: Show the question. If they miss it, briefly show them the right answer or definition.
Re-ask: Hide the answer right away, mix it back in, and ask the exact same question again.
Remember: Once they get it right, use a fun trick, connection, or praise to help them remember it.
Tip: Stuck or made a mistake? Give a small hint (Tip #1). Still stuck? Give a different hint (Tip #2).
Try Again: Let your partner try to solve the step again using those two helpful tips.
Teach: Still stuck? Explain and model how to do it. Then have them try a new one to prove they've got it!
